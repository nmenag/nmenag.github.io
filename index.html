Habit Money

Track expenses, plan monthly budgets, and reach savings goals with total privacy.

Habit Money is a professional, offline-first personal ledger designed to help you build active awareness of your spending habits. Unlike other tools, it requires zero bank integrations, cloud log-ins, or tracking pixels—guaranteeing 100% data ownership.

  • Absolute Privacy: All transactions are stored locally in an on-device SQLite database. No cloud storage.
  • Category Budgets: Establish monthly targets and track category progress with smart alerts.
  • Advanced Offline Charts: Track net cash flows and growth dynamics securely without remote telemetry skews.
